The centrepiece. Get this right and it feels like the box; get it wrong and nothing
else matters.

- Render EVERY player's timeline on EVERY screen. An opponent's row is not decorative;
  it is the information the steal is played on
- Active player's own row grows insertion slots between cards
- Tap a gap to place, not drag — must work one-handed on a phone
- Opponents' rows read-only but complete
- Reveal animation: flip the card, show the year, resolve

Mobile-first.
