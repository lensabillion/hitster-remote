Highest fun-per-line-of-code in the design.

Each player contributes ~15 songs before the first game. The deck becomes the group's
own music. After each reveal, show WHO added the song.

- Turns an abstract trivia question into a social one: "who put this in?"
- Solves cultural fairness directly — a deck each friend stocked personally cannot be
  one-sided
- Contributions stay hidden until reveal

Builds on the deck-builder CLI.
