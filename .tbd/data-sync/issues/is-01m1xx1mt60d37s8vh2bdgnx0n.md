Gartic Phone's lesson: the communal reveal is reliably the funniest moment of the
night. Do not quietly update state.

On every screen at once:
1. All sealed placements flip simultaneously
2. The year lands, big, with album art
3. Correct placements highlight; near-misses show how close
4. Steals resolve visibly ("Sara took that card from you")
5. Who added the song is revealed last

Worth more engineering attention than the placement UI.
