The honest answer to "I don't want to own a private song database".

Every year we confirm by hand is a fact that does not exist in any public database. If we
contribute those back — Discogs release entries, MusicBrainz release-groups, Wikidata
P577 — then:
- the data stops being a private asset and becomes something anyone can query
- our own database degrades into a cache rather than the source of truth
- Ethiopian music gets better documented, which is the actual root cause of this whole
  problem

Not urgent, and not free (each contribution needs care and citations). Worth doing once
the deck is real and the years have been played against a few times.
